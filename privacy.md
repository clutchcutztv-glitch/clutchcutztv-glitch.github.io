# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

clutchcutz poster ("the app") is a private tool run by clutchcutz. Its only purpose is to upload short videos that we made to the YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts that we own. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the clutchcutz team signs in to it.

## What the app can access

When we sign in to the app with our own accounts, YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta) give it permission to upload videos to those accounts. The app does not ask for, read or store anyone else's data.

## How information is used and stored

- Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer and are used only to upload our videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times.
- The app does not collect, sell, rent or share personal information.
- The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.
- We can delete the stored tokens at any time, and they are deleted if we stop using the app.

## YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## TikTok and Instagram

The app uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API under their developer terms. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok and Instagram app settings.
